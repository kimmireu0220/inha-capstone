Original-referenced portrait editing requires compiling accumulated requests into current editing conditions. We propose a method that extracts changes once and checks removal, restoration, preservation, and instruction eligibility. In an initial ten-step experiment on six synthetic people, original-referenced regeneration was closer to the source on all six final pairs using registered MAE, SSIM, and LPIPS. On twelve new synthetic English requests, applying the initial response directly matched 3/12 full reference states for both Qwen3-4B and Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct; the proposed method matched 12/12 for both. A follow-up comparison generated eighteen images from two real-person photographs and three dialogues. Compiling checked current conditions reduced the mean final prompt length from 204.00 to 122.67 tokens, a 39.87% reduction. A post-hoc Qwen2.5-VL-7B evaluator scored 34/36 target attributes for full request histories and 36/36 for checked conditions. Facial preservation metrics showed mixed directions across input forms. State-update evaluation used six editing attributes and supported English instruction forms in AI-generated synthetic dialogues.
