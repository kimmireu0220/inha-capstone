An initial comparison on six synthetic people found that original-referenced regeneration was closer to the source than ten-step sequential editing on all six final pairs using registered MAE, SSIM, and LPIPS. To compile current editing conditions for regeneration, we propose extracting changes once and checking restoration, preservation, removal, and instruction eligibility. On twelve new synthetic English requests, applying the initial response as a schema-only JSON patch matched 3/12 full reference states for both Qwen3-4B and Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct; the proposed method matched 12/12 for both. Combining extraction, model review, and the same checks matched 12/12 and 4/12, respectively. A follow-up experiment generated eighteen images from two real-person photographs using the final requests of three dialogues. Compiling checked current conditions reduced the mean prompt length from 204.00 to 122.67 tokens, a 39.87% reduction. A post-hoc 7B AI evaluator scored 34/36 target attributes for full request histories, 24/36 for unchecked states, and 36/36 for checked states. Facial preservation metrics showed mixed directions across input forms. Primary and secondary AI evaluation errors were retained. State-update evaluation covers six specified editing attributes and supported instruction forms in AI-generated synthetic English dialogues.
